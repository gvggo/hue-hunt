How to play

    
  • Match the target color using Hue, Saturation and Lightness sliders — no numbers, just your eyes.

    • 
    
  • Each guess shows 3 tiles: Hue · Saturation · Lightness. 🟩 close, 🟨 near, ⬛ far, with arrows (↑ more / ↓ less, ↻ ↺ for hue).
    • 
    
  • Everyone gets the same color each day. Your streak is saved only in this browser.
